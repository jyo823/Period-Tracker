Wellness Tips 💡

🧘

Gentle Exercise

Light yoga, walking, or stretching can help reduce cramps and boost mood. Try 15-20 minutes of gentle movement.

🫖

Warm Comfort

A heating pad on your lower abdomen or warm herbal tea can soothe cramps naturally. Chamomile and ginger are great choices.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drinking plenty of water helps reduce bloating and can ease headaches. Aim for 8 glasses throughout the day.

🥗

Nourishing Foods

Iron-rich foods like spinach and legumes help replace lost nutrients. Dark chocolate (in moderation!) can help with cravings.

😴

Rest & Sleep

Your body needs extra rest during your period. Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep and don't feel guilty about napping!

🎭

Managing Mood Swings

Hormonal changes are normal! Practice self-compassion, try deep breathing, and remember it's okay to feel your feelings.
